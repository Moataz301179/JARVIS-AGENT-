GUIDED RECOVERY

Reconnect safely, one verified layer at a time.

These are instructions and checkpoints. Restarting or changing any production system requires your explicit approval in the connected environment.

1

Connect the remote runtime

Authorize only the worker or service that should run independently of your Mac. Record its health endpoint and heartbeat interval.

2

Verify the scheduler

Register each task, its frequency, owner, timeout, and safe retry policy. Require at least one successful timestamped run.

3

Prove notification delivery

Choose your contact route, send a test, and store the provider’s delivery confirmation—not just a “sent” response.

4

Approve any corrective action

Review the failure evidence, proposed scope, rollback plan, and service impact before a restart or configuration change is permitted.
